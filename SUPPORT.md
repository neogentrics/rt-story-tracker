# Getting help

**In the app.** The Help menu has what's available, and *About* shows your version. Your version is the first thing we ask for
when something goes wrong.

**Something's wrong.** [Report a bug](https://github.com/neogentrics/rt-story-tracker/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
Say what happened, how to make it happen again, and your version. If it lost any writing, say so first: that's what we
fix first.

**You have an idea or a wish.** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/neogentrics/rt-story-tracker/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
Check the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) first, since it may already be planned.

**A question, or how it felt to use.** [Ask or tell us](https://github.com/neogentrics/rt-story-tracker/issues/new?template=question_or_feedback.yml).

**It's a security problem.** Please don't open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Keep your writing out of public issues.** This page is public. Describe a problem in your own words, or use made-up text
that shows the same thing. Never paste a password or recovery key.

RT Story Tracker is free, and support is on a best-effort basis.
